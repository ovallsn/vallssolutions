# Valls Solutions logo exploration

Four editable vector directions are shown in [the SVG board](assets/valls-logo-exploration.svg), with a high-resolution [PNG preview](assets/valls-logo-exploration.png). The selected direction has a separate [Single Ribbon refinement board](assets/valls-single-ribbon-refinements.svg) and [PNG preview](assets/valls-single-ribbon-refinements.png).

1. **Open Threshold** — closest to the original V. Broad, balanced legs and a central terracotta detail suggest a new structure taking shape.
2. **Single Ribbon** — the most reduced silhouette, with a restrained copper edge. **Selected** for the clearest small-size V and simplest navbar/favicon use.
3. **Form & Frame** — an open architectural frame that resolves into a V. Clear but more literal and less distinctive.
4. **V Signature** — a compact copper stroke inside the V. Most individual at large sizes; its detail is less clear at favicon scale.

The selected direction has three small accent refinements. Variant B is the active choice: its copper detail is about 12.5% wider toward the inner edge, while the outer V stays unchanged. Variant A is the reference shape; variant C keeps the original accent width and shifts it inward. Each includes icon-only sizes, a horizontal wordmark, monochrome treatment and reverse-color application.

Palette: deep green #174c3e, terracotta #b85f43, warm paper #f8f6f0. The wordmark uses an editorial serif to sit comfortably beside the site's serif headings.
