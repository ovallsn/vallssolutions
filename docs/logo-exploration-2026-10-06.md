# Valls Solutions logo exploration

Four editable vector directions are shown in [the SVG board](assets/valls-logo-exploration.svg), with a high-resolution [PNG preview](assets/valls-logo-exploration.png).

1. **Open Threshold** — closest to the current V. Broad, balanced legs and a central terracotta detail suggest a new structure taking shape. Recommended overall.
2. **Single Ribbon** — the most reduced silhouette, with a restrained copper edge. Strong alternative when small-size simplicity is the priority.
3. **Form & Frame** — an open architectural frame that resolves into a V. Clear but more literal and less distinctive.
4. **V Signature** — a compact copper stroke inside the V. Most individual at large sizes; its detail is less clear at favicon scale.

Each direction includes an icon-only sample at several small sizes, a horizontal “Valls Solutions” wordmark, a monochrome treatment, and a reverse-color application on deep green. These are design options for review; the active website mark remains unchanged until a direction is selected.

Palette: deep green #174c3e, terracotta #b85f43, warm paper #f8f6f0. The wordmark uses an editorial serif to sit comfortably beside the site's serif headings.
