# Premium marketing redesign — 7 October 2026

## Brief and decisions

Audience: U.S. and international founders, freelancers, agencies and online business owners. The primary action remains an initial enquiry by business email. The owner delegated design and content decisions and authorized publication directly to main.

The existing Next.js/React/TypeScript static export, bilingual routes, V mark, green/copper/paper colors and normal CSS are retained. The redesign changes the composition and message hierarchy: a clear LLC formation hero, a visible total price, concrete business uses, a detailed service list, experience and direct support, process, banking preparation and an editorial resource section. No new runtime dependencies were added.

## References inspected

- [doola](https://www.doola.com/): founder-focused headline and visible next action, with a visual service explanation. Adapt the clarity, not its yellow branding, collage or claims.
- [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas): concise setup proposition, deliberate split composition and emphasis on deliverables. Valls uses its own service summary rather than copying proprietary product screenshots.
- [Clerky](https://www.clerky.com/): simple hierarchy and strong focus on the company's service. Valls uses a warmer editorial system and a more specific bundled offer.
- [Northwest Registered Agent](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/incorporation-service): concrete formation scope and human support. Valls presents its confirmed bilingual package and renewal price.
- [Jiro](https://jiro.build/): consulted as a section-reference directory. No template, source code or assets were reused.

Reference screenshots are local review artifacts outside the published site. Competitor visuals, reviews, logos, assets and copy are not incorporated; there is no third-party template code requiring a new license notice.

The selected direction uses deep green for the initial proposition and banking section, warm paper for reading, and copper for restrained accents. The service section separates the inclusion list from the initial/renewal price summary. Real trust signals are limited to the owner's supplied 100+ LLC formations, published scope and direct bilingual communication.

## Content and SEO

Added three paired English/Spanish guides with descriptive canonical slugs:

- Choosing an LLC formation service: how to compare scope, support and renewal.
- Business bank account preparation: documents, business description and address distinctions.
- Wyoming annual renewal: reporting calendar, included maintenance and website handover.

Expanded the LLC benefits guide with consultant, agency and online-business examples. The blog now groups formation, operations and tax/international planning topics. Article links, localized counterparts, canonical metadata and sitemap entries continue to come from the existing route and article registries. Existing legacy redirects remain supported.

Primary official sources checked:

- [Wyoming business FAQs](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS): Annual Report due date and asset-based license tax.
- [IRS LLC information](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc): entity and federal classification context.
- [SBA launch guidance](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/launch-your-business/): structure, liability and business-location context.
- [Mercury eligibility](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770467511060-Eligibility-and-requirements-for-opening-a-Mercury-account) and [document checklist](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770957425172-Gathering-your-documents): bank application preparation.

No tax savings, account approval, visa outcome, processing deadline, ranking or competitor traffic is guaranteed. $699 formation and $449 annual renewal retain the owner's confirmed scope. Tax preparation, legal advice and an Operating Agreement are not added to the package. Business email renewal terms are not invented.

## Media, accessibility and delivery

The existing self-hosted hero video and poster are retained under their existing project media record. They are presented as illustrative footage, not as the Valls team or clients. Poster-first loading, reduced-motion/data-saving handling, pause control and fallback remain in the existing HeroVideo component. No external credit is added to customer-facing copy.

New marketing sections are server-rendered. No animation library, fake document, fake dashboard or client data collection is introduced. Native FAQ disclosures, semantic headings, visible focus and responsive layouts are retained. The future customer portal remains configurable and hidden until a real URL exists.

Validation includes TypeScript, the existing catalog tests, static production build, exported SEO audit, desktop/tablet/mobile browser review and checks of contact links, navigation, language switching and FAQ behavior. There is no configured lint command. Search Console metrics and measured Core Web Vitals are unavailable in this task; no ranking or field-performance claim is made.
