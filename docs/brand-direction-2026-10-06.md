# Valls Solutions — logo direction

## Selected direction

Use **02 — Single Ribbon** as the active mark. Keep its recognizable outer V and the editorial serif wordmark “Valls Solutions.” The mark uses deep green `#174c3e`, terracotta `#b85f43` and warm paper `#f8f6f0`; on dark backgrounds, use cream with a softened copper accent.

The final refinement uses a copper detail widened by about 12.5% toward the inside of the V. This gives the accent more presence at small sizes while keeping the V silhouette and its outer edge unchanged. The crop in the icon assets is optically tightened around the symbol.

## Ranking and rejected directions

The owner's ranking is **02 > 01 > 03 > 04**. Single Ribbon is clearest at favicon and navigation size. Direction 01 has more character but its inner terracotta detail compresses when small. Direction 03 can read as a U or shield. Direction 04's curved accent becomes decorative and competes with the V.

No globes, arrows, buildings, stars or “LLC” are part of the chosen mark. This keeps it focused on the company name and avoids an overly literal industry symbol.

## Use

- Navigation: transparent vector V beside the accessible HTML wordmark “Valls Solutions” in a refined serif.
- Footer: reverse-color V and wordmark on the existing deep background.
- Favicon: the same Single Ribbon geometry in a tightly cropped vector.
- Social previews: the same mark and serif wordmark, with promotional text kept as editable SVG text.
- Email signature and documents: transparent horizontal SVG and PNG versions; use the monochrome vector for one-ink print where appropriate.

These are original vector paths drawn for Valls Solutions. This design choice is not trademark clearance or a claim that the mark is unique in every market.
