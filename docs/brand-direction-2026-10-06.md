# Valls Solutions — logo direction

## Recommendation
Use the new V monogram with the existing text wordmark “Valls Solutions.” It pairs with the current site rather than adding a separate, mismatched luxury-finance identity. The mark suggests the company taking shape and a way forward; green anchors it and the small terracotta element gives it warmth.

## Review of the four supplied directions

1. Globe + orbit + star + V: signals international reach, but the orbit, continents and star become busy at navigation and favicon sizes. It can read as a travel or logistics brand.
2. V + skyscraper + orbit: the most literal but looks like a property developer, architecture firm or investment company.
3. V + globe + rising bars: strong color and recognizable initials, but the bars emphasize investment/growth and the orbit makes it a crowded financial-services mark.
4. VS monogram: simplest and best starting point. Refine it by removing the metallic treatment and pairing it with the site's existing humanist typography and colors.

## Chosen use
- Navigation: transparent V symbol beside accessible HTML text “Valls Solutions.”
- Favicon: small vector V in forest green and terracotta.
- Social previews: same palette and simplified symbol; keep all promotional text as editable SVG text.
- Colors: site forest green `#174c3e`, terracotta `#b85f43`, warm paper background `#f8f6f0`.
- Keep the symbol readable by itself; do not add tiny service descriptors to the icon.

The icon was generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-06, then cropped to its transparent bounds for the web. This is a first working brand direction, not a trademark clearance or a claim that the mark is unique in every market. The original generated image remains in Codex generated assets.
