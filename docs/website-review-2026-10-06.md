Video has a poster, pause control, error fallback and reduced-motion handling. Media provenance stays in docs/assets/media-sources.md, not promotional copy.

## Search/content plan
- Home: U.S. LLC formation for domestic and international founders; clear offer and email CTA.
- LLC formation: scope, owner situation, EIN, banking boundaries and renewal.
- Pricing: formation versus renewal, actual inclusions and state-fee treatment.
- Guides: Wyoming suitability, LLC uses, taxation, bank applications, visas and EIN versus ITIN. Keep tax/visa claims conditional and link primary sources.
- New bilingual EIN/ITIN guide connects formation, IRS requirements, pricing and tax guidance. Internal article language switching uses the shared catalog.
