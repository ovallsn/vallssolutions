The right LLC formation service should make the next steps easy to understand: what gets filed, what you receive, who helps you and what happens a year later. Compare the whole service before comparing its headline price.

## Start with what you want the company to do

A freelancer starting to contract through a company, an agency working with international clients and a founder operating a local U.S. business may need different support. Write down your activity, where you live, where the business operates and who will own it. These details give your provider a useful starting point.

For a U.S. founder working in a particular state, state registration requirements deserve attention before choosing a different formation state. For an international founder, the EIN application and business account preparation often become central practical questions.

## Compare a complete first-year package

A state filing is one part of getting a company organized. Ask each service to explain:

- Whether the state formation fee is included in the advertised total.
- Who handles the EIN application and what confirmation you receive.
- How long Registered Agent service and a mailing address are included.
- Whether website and business email setup are available.
- What banking guidance involves and who submits the application.
- How you contact someone when a question comes up.

A low entry price can be useful when you want only the filing. A broader package can make sense when you want to coordinate several essentials with one contact. Compare like-for-like scope and the cost of services you actually plan to use.
