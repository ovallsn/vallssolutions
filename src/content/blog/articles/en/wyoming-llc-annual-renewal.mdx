## When is the Wyoming Annual Report due?

Wyoming generally requires the report on the first day of the formation anniversary month. A company formed on October 15 has an October 1 annual due date. The license tax starts at $60 and increases for Wyoming assets above the applicable threshold. See the [Wyoming Secretary of State’s guidance](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS) for the calculation and filing rules.

The first report is part of the first annual renewal, rather than a filing made when the company is initially formed. Plan around the state deadline, which may fall before the exact anniversary day.
