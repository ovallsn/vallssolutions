You can own a U.S. LLC while living outside the United States. For freelancers, agencies and online business owners, it can provide an American company entity for contracts, business records and applications to financial platforms. Here is how to prepare a Wyoming LLC from abroad.

## Can a non-U.S. resident own an LLC?

The IRS explains that most states do not restrict LLC ownership to U.S. citizens or residents. You can generally form a Wyoming LLC without moving to the United States. State registration creates the company; your residence, activity and ownership determine the other requirements to review.

Our [guide to LLC benefits](/blog/do-i-need-an-llc/) explains what the structure can offer to a business. This article focuses on the practical steps for founders abroad.

## Prepare the company and owner information

Start with your preferred company name, a short description of the business, the owners and their ownership percentages. Identify who will manage the company and who will be the responsible party for the EIN application. Your foreign residential address and the company's Wyoming mailing address have different purposes and should be recorded accurately.

## Apply for the EIN after formation

An EIN identifies the business for federal tax and business purposes. An ITIN identifies an individual for certain tax purposes; they are different numbers. You do not need an ITIN simply to form the LLC.

The IRS provides application routes for international applicants, including Form SS-4. The method depends on the principal place of business and responsible-party information; the online route is not available to every founder. We handle the EIN application as part of the formation package. The IRS issues the number and determines processing time.

Read the [EIN and ITIN guide](/blog/ein-vs-itin/) for the distinction and application steps.

## Set up Registered Agent and mailing services

Your Wyoming LLC needs a Registered Agent in the state to receive official correspondence. The package also includes a Wyoming mailing address for business correspondence. A mailing address serves a different function from the physical location where you work or manage the company.
