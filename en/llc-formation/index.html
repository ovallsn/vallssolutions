What is included

An administrative launch with every step explained.

The $699 price is the total initial price to form a Wyoming LLC. It includes Wyoming’s state formation fee and the initial services listed below.

  • Preparation and filing of your Wyoming LLC formation.
  • EIN processing.
  • Registered Agent service for the first year.

The bank account is not part of LLC formation. We help you prepare; you submit the application and the financial provider decides whether to accept the business and its owners.

What happens from year two?

Renewal costs $449 per year and includes Registered Agent renewal, the Wyoming mailing address and filing of the Annual Report. Wyoming’s minimum state annual fee starts at $60 and may vary if the LLC has assets located in Wyoming.

The EIN process depends on the responsible party

The IRS offers different ways to apply for an EIN. Requirements may depend on where the business is located and the responsible party’s information. An ITIN is not a general prerequisite just to form an LLC; EIN application details are reviewed for each case.

Wyoming is not automatically right for everyone

If you live and operate in another U.S. state, you may need to register the LLC there or meet additional requirements. We do not recommend Wyoming by default. Consult a qualified professional for advice about your specific legal or tax situation.

A mailing address does not guarantee a bank account

The included address is a business mailing address. It does not mean you have a physical office or that a bank will accept it as your operating address. Always review each financial provider’s current requirements.
