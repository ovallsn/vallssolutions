Frequently asked questions

Answers before you begin.

Do I need to be a U.S. citizen to form an LLC?

No. U.S. and international founders can form an LLC. EIN information and bank requirements depend on each situation and provider. Do not email SSNs, ITINs or identity documents.

Will you open a bank account for me?

No. We guide you through the documents and application preparation; you submit the application and the bank makes the decision. We cannot guarantee an account or acceptance of your country or address.

Can I use a Wyoming mailing address to open an account?

Not necessarily. A mailing or Registered Agent address is not the same as a physical operating address. Each financial provider sets its own requirements. See, for example, the published requirements from Mercury and Wise.

Is Wyoming always the best state?

No. Your business location, residence and other factors may affect where you need to register or maintain a company. This page shows Wyoming pricing; we confirm the total for another state before work begins.

What does the $449 annual renewal include?

From year two, it includes Registered Agent renewal, the Wyoming mailing address and filing of the Wyoming Annual Report. It does not include federal or state tax returns or individual tax advice.

Is an Operating Agreement included?

It is not part of the package currently advertised. We will confirm any additional service before you purchase it.
